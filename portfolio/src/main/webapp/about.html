About me

Bringing digital canvases to life.


Hi, I am Erika. As a passionate student, developer, and creative thinker, I bridge the gap between strict logic and artistic vision. I love transforming complex code into warm, welcoming, and beautifully crafted digital experiences. My main goal is to build accessible web interfaces that feel genuinely human, turning ideas into perfectly functional interactive realities.

I.I.S. E.Fermi

My journey started here. After my first two years at an art school, I took a leap into the world of computer science. Coming from a surprisingly "analog" background, the transition wasn't easy—I barely even knew how to type at first! Yet, this place quickly became special to me. It’s where I built meaningful connections, began to discover my true self, and finally realized what I wanted to do.

UNISA

My adventure continued at university, where I strive to grow and sculpt my skills every single day.
